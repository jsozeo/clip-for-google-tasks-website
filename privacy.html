Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 June 2026 · Effective date: 21 June 2026

This privacy policy describes how the browser extension Clip for Google Tasks ("the Extension", "we", "us") accesses, uses, stores, and shares data — including Google user data — when you use it. The Extension is published by ozeo (Jean-Sébastien Mancebo), 14 avenue de l'Europe, 77144 Montévrain, France. Contact: contact@ozeo.io.

The Extension is a third-party tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC. Google Tasks™ is a trademark of Google LLC.

1. What the Extension does

Clip for Google Tasks is a client for Google Tasks. It lets you:

  • Select text on any web page and send it to your Google Tasks account as a new task, with a "scroll-to-text" deep link pointing back to the exact passage on the original page.
  • View, search, edit, and mark complete your existing Google Tasks from the Extension's popup and side panel, without leaving the page you are on.

Everything the Extension does happens directly between your browser and Google, using an OAuth access token you grant. The Extension is operated entirely on your device.

2. Google user data we access

With your authorization, the Extension uses the Google Tasks API (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks) to access the following Google user data:

  • Your task lists — their names and identifiers, so the Extension can show them and let you choose where a task goes.
  • Your tasks — their title, notes, due date, status, and completion date, so the Extension can display and manage them.
  • Create, edit, and complete permissions — to create new tasks and to update or mark complete tasks you choose to change.
  • The email address of the Google account you authorize — read once from your OAuth token. It is used only to build correct ?authuser= deep links so that links to your tasks open in the right Google account when you use multiple accounts. The email address itself is never transmitted to ozeo; only its coarse domain may appear in anonymous analytics (see section 4). It is never used for advertising.

3. How we use this data (Data Usage)

  • Reading your tasks is used to display them in the Extension's popup and side panel so you can view, search, and manage them.
  • Text you select plus the current page's URL and title are used to create a new Google Tasks task with a scroll-to-text deep link back to the source.
  • Edits and completions you make in the side panel are written back to your Google Tasks account.
  • Labels are stored inline inside each task's own notes field (inside your Google account, via a small marker line) so the Extension can render colored tags. No separate label store leaves your account.
  • Your account email is used solely to construct correct multi-account deep links, as described above.

The Extension does not read the content of pages you visit unless you explicitly invoke it (by clipping a selection or using an on-page action), and it does not track your browsing history.

4. How we share this data (Data Sharing)

The only recipient of your Google user data (your task lists, your tasks, and their content) is Google's own Google Tasks API (googleapis.com). That data stays in your own Google Tasks account, governed by Google's privacy policy. It is never sent to ozeo, and ozeo never sells, rents, or shares it with any third party.

To understand how the Extension is used and to improve it, ozeo collects anonymous, opt-out usage analytics on its own server (an AWS-hosted endpoint in the EU — not a third-party analytics provider). Each event contains only:

  • a random install identifier generated locally on your device (not tied to your name, email, or Google account);
  • the event name (e.g. clip_created, dashboard_opened) and the extension version;
  • a coarse site category for clips (e.g. jira_confluence, gmail, other) — never the actual URL, page title, or the text you selected;
  • the coarse domain of your account email (e.g. gmail.com, company.com), used only to distinguish personal from organizational usage. The email address itself is never transmitted.

These analytics never include your tasks, notes, labels, full URLs, page content, or your email address. They involve no advertising, no third-party trackers, and no cookies. You can turn them off at any time from the Extension's Options page ("Help improve Clip by sharing anonymous usage stats").

5. How we store and protect this data (Data Storage & Protection)

  • Your tasks live on Google's servers, inside your own Google account, under Google's privacy policy. The Extension does not keep its own copy on any server.
  • On your device only, the Extension keeps small caches via Chrome's storage API:
    • Your preferences (default task list, default due-date offset) and your label colors, in chrome.storage.sync — synchronized across your own Chrome installations by Chrome, via your Google account.
    • The resolved account email, in chrome.storage.local, used to build the deep links described in section 2.
    • An OAuth access token, managed by Chrome's identity API, for the duration of a session.
  • All communication with Google APIs uses HTTPS. We request the minimum scope necessary (see section 6) and we hold no server-side copy of your data.
  • You can revoke the Extension's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Uninstalling the Extension removes all of its local caches from your device.

6. Requesting minimum scopes

OAuth scope requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks. This is the minimum scope required for the Extension's core functions, because it both creates tasks (from web clips) and lets you view, edit, and complete your existing tasks in the side panel. A read-only Tasks scope would not allow creating, editing, or completing tasks, and Google offers no narrower "create-only" Tasks scope. The Extension requests no other Google API scopes.

8. Data retention

ozeo retains no data related to your use of the Extension, because none of your data ever reaches our systems. All of your task data lives in your own Google account and is retained by Google under your control. Local caches on your device persist until you clear them or uninstall the Extension.

9. Future paid features

We may, in the future, offer optional paid features (the "Paid Plan"). The Paid Plan will be opt-in: you are never required to subscribe to use the free version of the Extension described above.

If and when you choose to subscribe to a Paid Plan:

  • You will be asked to create an account on clip.ozeo.io using "Sign in with Google", which will share with us your email address and basic profile information (name, profile picture) — and nothing else from your Google account.
  • Payment processing will be handled by Stripe. We will not see or store your full payment card details. Stripe will store the billing information necessary to process recurring payments, governed by Stripe's privacy policy.
  • We will store: your email, your subscription status, and aggregated usage counters (e.g. number of clips this month) for quota enforcement. This data lives in our own AWS-hosted database, in the EU region.
  • Any premium feature that involves server-side processing will be described and explicitly opted into before any data is sent to our servers, and this policy will be updated accordingly before it launches.

These future features are not active at the time of this policy's publication. When they become active, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

10. Your rights under GDPR (RGPD)

As a user located in the European Union or the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your data, data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in France: CNIL).

For the free Extension: because we do not store your data, these rights are exercised directly with Google. You can manage your Google Tasks data, revoke the Extension's access, and export or delete your tasks from your Google Account.

For the (future) Paid Plan: these rights are exercised by contacting us at contact@ozeo.io.
