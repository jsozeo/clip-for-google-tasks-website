Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 May 2026 · Effective date: 24 May 2026

This privacy policy describes how the browser extension Clip for Google Tasks ("the Extension", "we", "us") handles data when you use it. The Extension is published by ozeo (Jean-Sébastien Mancebo), 14 avenue de l'Europe, 77144 Montévrain, France. Contact: contact@ozeo.io.

The Extension is a third-party tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google LLC. Google Tasks™ is a trademark of Google LLC.

1. What the Extension does

The Extension lets you select text on any web page and send it to your Google Tasks account as a new task, with a "scroll-to-text" deep link pointing back to the exact passage on the original page.

2. What data the Extension accesses

The Extension only accesses data at the moment you explicitly trigger it by right-clicking selected text and choosing "Clip to Google Tasks". At that moment, it reads:

  • The text you selected
  • The URL of the current page
  • The title of the current page

This data is used exclusively to build a Google Tasks task on your behalf, in your own Google Tasks account.

The Extension does not:

  • Read the content of pages you visit when you do not invoke it
  • Track your browsing history
  • Send any data to ozeo or to any third party other than Google Tasks API
  • Use analytics, cookies, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting
  • Sell, rent, or share data with anyone

3. Data sent to Google

When you click "Clip", the task data (title, notes, optional due date, list selection) is sent directly from your browser to the Google Tasks API (googleapis.com), using an OAuth access token granted by you. The data ends up in your own Google Tasks account, governed by Google's privacy policy.

ozeo never sees this data and has no access to it.

4. Data stored locally

The Extension stores the following in your browser's chrome.storage.sync area (synchronised across your Chrome installations via your Google account, by Chrome):

  • Your default Google Tasks list (if set in the Options page)
  • Your default due-date offset (if set in the Options page)

This data stays inside your Google account and is never transmitted to ozeo.

The Extension also caches an OAuth access token (managed by Chrome's identity API) for the duration of a session. You can revoke this token at any time from your Google Account permissions page or from the Extension's Options page.

5. Future paid features

We may, in the future, offer optional paid features (the "Paid Plan"). The Paid Plan will be opt-in: you are never required to subscribe to use the free version of the Extension described above.

If and when you choose to subscribe to a Paid Plan:

  • You will be asked to create an account on clip.ozeo.io using "Sign in with Google", which will share with us your email address and basic profile information (name, profile picture) — and nothing else from your Google account.
  • Payment processing will be handled by Stripe. We will not see or store your full payment card details. Stripe will store the billing information necessary to process recurring payments, governed by Stripe's privacy policy.
  • We will store: your email, your subscription status, your aggregated usage counters (e.g. number of clips this month) for quota enforcement. This data lives in our own AWS-hosted database, in the EU region.
  • Any premium features that involve server-side processing (such as automatic enrichment of clipped data) will be described and explicitly opted into before any data is sent to our servers.

These future features are not active at the time of this policy's publication. When they become active, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.

6. Permissions, justified

Permission Why
contextMenus To add the "Clip to Google Tasks" item to the right-click menu
identity To obtain an OAuth token to call the Google Tasks API on your behalf
storage To remember your default list and due-date offset preferences
https://www.googleapis.com/* To call the Google Tasks API

OAuth scope requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks. This is the minimum scope required to create tasks in your Google Tasks account.

7. Data retention

ozeo retains no data related to your use of the free version of the Extension, because no data ever reaches our systems. All data lives in your own Google account.

For the (future) Paid Plan, we will retain your account data for as long as your subscription is active, plus the legally required retention period after termination (typically up to 10 years for billing records under French tax law). You can request deletion of your account at any time; non-mandatory data is then erased within 30 days.

8. Your rights under GDPR (RGPD)

As a user located in the European Union or the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Request erasure
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in France: CNIL)

For the free Extension: because we do not store your data, these rights are exercised directly with Google. You can manage your Google Tasks data, revoke the Extension's access, and export or delete your tasks from your Google Account.

For the (future) Paid Plan: these rights are exercised by contacting us at contact@ozeo.io.

9. Children

The Extension is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the Extension's listing on the Chrome Web Store and via this page. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.

11. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

Clip for Google Tasks's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
